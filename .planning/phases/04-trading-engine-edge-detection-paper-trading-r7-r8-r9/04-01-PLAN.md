<objective>
Build the trading logic: an edge detector comparing model probabilities to Kalshi
market prices across all market types, a fractional-Kelly position sizer with
per-type caps and correlation limits, a risk manager with daily loss limits and a
kill switch, an order manager submitting maker-biased limit orders to the Kalshi
demo API, and a paper trading ledger recording everything to SQLite.

Purpose: This is the layer that turns Phase 3's calibrated probabilities into
actual (paper) trades, with the risk controls required before any real capital
is ever at stake.
Output: edge_detector.py, position_sizer.py, risk_manager.py, order_manager.py, ledger.py.
</objective>

<what-was-built>

**src/trading/edge_detector.py** — `EdgeDetector(config)`. `calculate_edge()` computes `model_prob - market_price` (R7.2). `evaluate_market()` applies per-market-type minimum edge thresholds (R7.3: 5c games / 3c weather / 7c props via `_get_min_edge`), liquidity filters via bid-ask spread and volume (R7.4: `_get_max_spread`, `_get_min_volume`), and confidence filtering (R7.5). `rank_signals()` scores by `edge x sqrt(liquidity) x confidence` (R7.6). `scan_and_rank()` is the top-level entry point producing structured `Signal` objects (R7.7).

**src/trading/position_sizer.py** — `PositionSizer(config)`. `calculate_kelly()` implements 0.25x fractional Kelly (R8.1). `size_position()` applies per-market-type caps via `_get_per_type_cap` (R8.2: 50 games / 100 weather / 25 props), portfolio exposure cap (R8.3), and correlation caps via `count_same_game_positions()` / `count_same_city_weather()` (R8.6: max 3 same-game, max 2 same-city-weather).

**src/trading/risk_manager.py** — `RiskManager(config)`, `KillSwitchTriggered` exception. `check_daily_loss()` raises on breach of the daily loss limit (R8.4). `check_position_limit()` / `check_exposure_limit()` enforce max concurrent positions (R8.5). `pre_trade_check()` gates every order through both. `emergency_halt(kalshi_client)` cancels all open orders and sets a halt flag; `reset_halt()` clears it.

**src/trading/order_manager.py** — `OrderManager(kalshi_client, ledger)`. `submit_order(sized, dry_run=False)` converts a `SizedOrder` into a Kalshi limit order with `post_only=True` (maker bias, consistent with Phase 1 decision D-02), records the attempt to the ledger regardless of outcome. `cancel_all_open()` and `check_fills()` round out order lifecycle tracking (R9.5).

**src/trading/ledger.py** — `Ledger(db)`. `record_trade()`, `record_prediction()`, `record_outcome()`, `update_trade_status()`, `update_daily_pnl()` write to the `trades`/`predictions`/`outcomes`/`daily_pnl` tables (already present in `schema.sql` from Phase 1, satisfying R9.1's 8-table requirement). `get_daily_pnl()`, `get_trades()`, `get_total_pnl()`, `get_trade_count()` provide the read side for Phase 5 analytics (R9.2-R9.4, R9.6).

</what-was-built>
